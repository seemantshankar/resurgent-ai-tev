# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

R.K. Associates & Hoteliers Private Limited operates a **critical premium train services business** for the Indian Railways ecosystem, managing high-margin tourism and premium passenger segments including Rajdhani Special Deluxe (RSD), Mail Express, and Bharat Gaurav trains.

## Key Investment Highlights

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Historical Revenue CAGR (FY 23–27)** | 59.4% | Exceptional growth trajectory |
| **FY 2027 Revenue** | ₹29,399 Million | ₹2,940 Crores audited |
| **FY 2027 EBITDA Margin** | 11.2% | Strong, stable operations |
| **Interest Coverage (EBITDA/Int)** | 4.7x | Far exceeds 2.5x covenant |
| **Debt Service Coverage Ratio** | 3.0x | Excellent debt service capacity |
| **Current Debt Outstanding** | ₹4,230 Crores | Declining trajectory |
| **5-Year Debt Reduction Target** | ₹1,810 Crores | 57% deleveraging by Mar 2031 |
| **Syndication Facility** | ₹300–400 Crores | For train procurement + refinance |
| **Credit Rating (Internal)** | BB+ (Stable) | Mid-market growth company |

## Investment Thesis

**Why approve this syndication:**

1. **Government-Mandated Business:** Long-term Ministry of Railways concessions for premium train operations (regulatory moat)
2. **Exceptional Growth:** 59.4% CAGR with improving margins (EBITDA elasticity 2.1x in FY 2027)
3. **Strong Debt Service:** ICR 4.7x, DSCR 3.0x, interest declining despite revenue growth
4. **Committed Deleveraging:** Debt falling ₹4,230 Cr → ₹1,810 Cr while expanding operations
5. **Portfolio Diversification:** Dual business (RKA existing + BFP new) reduces single-point risk
6. **Secular Growth Driver:** Rising middle class, leisure travel boom, government tourism push
7. **Resilient to Shocks:** Stress tests show 47% revenue buffer before covenant breach

### Inter-Lender Agreement & Pari Passu Mechanics

**Definition - Pari Passu Charge:**
Pari passu (Latin: "with equal step") means all lenders have **equal ranking and priority** over the charged collateral. In case of default:
- Proceeds from collateral liquidation are distributed **pro-rata** based on outstanding balance
- No single lender has priority over another (all are co-equal)
- Protects smaller co-lenders from super-priority claims

**For This Syndication:**

**TLA & TLB Pari Passu Structure on Current Assets:**
| Aspect | Detail | Banker Protection |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Collateral Pool** | Current Assets: Receivables (₹1,469M) + Inventory (₹257M) = ₹1,726M | 4.3x – 5.8x coverage vs ₹300–400M facility |
| **Lender Ranking** | All syndication lenders have equal/pari passu claim | No subordination; fair sharing |
| **Pro-Rata Distribution** | If CA realizes ₹500M in liquidation: TLA gets ₹100M, TLB gets ₹400M (based on exposure) | Mechanical, no negotiation required |
| **Priority Over Third Parties** | Pari passu lenders > Unsecured creditors; > Equity holders | Senior security position |
| **Insurance Proceeds** | Loss payable clause ensures insurance proceeds flow to security pool | No insurance gap |

**Inter-Lender Agreement (ILA) – Standard Provisions:**

| Provision | Purpose | Borrower Benefit | Lender Benefit |
|-----------|---------|----------|----------|
| **Standstill Clause** | Lenders cannot act unilaterally; 90-day notice required before enforcement | Prevents sudden enforcement surprise | Forces negotiated resolution |
| **Voting Majority** | Decisions require 51–75% of lender group consent (depending on matter) | Borrower knows who to negotiate with | Prevents single lender veto |
| **No Subordination** | Each lender maintains pari passu status throughout loan tenor | No hidden pyramiding of claims | Equal safety for all |
| **Sharing of Proceeds** | Any prepayment or collateral recovery shared pro-rata | Transparent, predictable | Fair distribution |
| **Cross-Default** | Default to one lender = default to all (standard) | Know full obligation | Common protection |
| **Acceleration Coordination** | Acceleration vote requires majority (not unanimous) | Prevents holdout veto | Democratic enforcement |

**Subordination Hierarchy (This Facility):**

```
1. STATUTORY OBLIGATIONS
   ├─ Employee wages
   ├─ Statutory dues (taxes, GST, labor)
   └─ Secured creditor claims per law

2. SECURED LENDERS (Syndicate)
   ├─ TLA: First legal charge on trains (₹150–180 Cr) [SENIOR]
   │   └─ 100% collateral backing; exclusive security
   │
   ├─ TLB + WC: Pari passu on Current Assets (₹150–220 Cr combined) [CO-EQUAL]
   │   └─ Both rank equally; pro-rata distribution on CA
   │
   └─ Parent Guarantee: RK Group backing (₹1,522M equity) [JUNIOR to secured]

3. UNSECURED CREDITORS
   ├─ Trade payables (₹828M per FY 2025)
   ├─ Accrued expenses
   └─ Unsecured lenders

4. EQUITY HOLDERS
   └─ Residual claim after all obligations paid
```
