# Autonomous web-grounded pack induction and single-document generalization

The re-classification of an induced pack against heading phrases, and the assumption of one document type per file, are superseded by [ADR 0031](0031-read-each-document-from-its-contents.md). Induction of a new kind of document, single-document generalisation, and pack widening stand.

In M2, `PackInducer` and `Generalizer` were built around multi-document corpus clustering (`clusterDocCount >= 2`): `Generalizer` dropped every non-spine field when `clusterDocCount == 1`, `LlmPackMiningClient` inspected only the first 1,500 characters of raw text without domain or web grounding, `PackInducer` hardcoded empty lists for `tables`, `clauses`, and `passages`, `DocTypeClassifier` filtered out candidate packs whenever `file_role` did not match, and `DocExtractRunner` threw an exception whenever a document remained `unknown`. In practice, TEV and Debt Syndication mandates regularly introduce document types on a single-instance basis (`N = 1`) — including Bank Statements, Resumes/Director Profiles, Credit Rating Reports (CRISIL/ICRA/CARE/India Ratings), Debt Structuring Term Sheets and Information Memoranda, CIBIL/Bureau default screenshots, Land Lease/Sale Deeds, Vendor Quotations, and specialized technical reports. Requiring manual YAML pack creation or multi-document batches causes the pipeline to fail whenever a new industry or mandate document arrives.

**Decision.**

1. **Zero-touch on-demand induction (`classify → auto-induce → extract`).** When `tev-doc classify` or `tev-doc extract` encounters a document version that does not match any existing seed or induced pack above threshold (`doc_type_code = 'unknown'`), the pipeline automatically runs `PackInducer` on the unclassified document(s), publishes the induced pack to `PackStore` and `doc_type_pack`, re-classifies the document against the newly minted pack, and proceeds directly to extraction and nomenclature binding without human intervention. `DocTypeClassifier` treats `file_role` as a soft tie-breaker boost rather than a hard filter so generic or mismatched intake roles never hide a matching pack.

2. **Sanitized web-grounded domain discovery (`PackMiningClient`).** When inducing or widening a pack (and when mandate `data_residency` is `cloud_allowed`), `LlmPackMiningClient` extracts a sanitized structural fingerprint from the document's full multi-page headings, table column headers, and layout blocks — stripping company/person names, PAN/CIN/GSTIN/account numbers, and currency amounts via `EntityTokenFilter` (preserving ADR 0008 confidentiality). It queries a web-search-grounded LLM or search tool using only generic document-type and industry/syndication terms to identify what the document represents in TEV or Debt Syndication and what scalar fields, schedule tables (Record schedules and Period/Dimension matrices), legal/financial clauses, and narrative passages analysts require. The sanitized search query and domain rationale are recorded in `induction_decision`.

3. **Single-document (`N = 1`) generalization and entity-token hygiene.** `Generalizer` no longer drops domain fields solely because `clusterDocCount == 1`. A candidate field, table, clause, or passage in a single-document cluster is promoted when:
   - Its key, path, aliases, and identity signals pass `EntityTokenFilter` (label-not-value: no borrower/promoter/lender names, identifiers, or literal amounts).
   - Its `path` sits under one of the 7 frozen spine roots (`identity`, `economic`, `technical`, `commercial`, `legal`, `regulatory`, `frame`), backed by an expanded `SpineCatalog` covering both TEV and Debt Syndication concepts (credit ratings, debt facility tranches/pricing/covenants, bureau DPD/CMR/SMA delinquency, bank statement cash-flow summaries, and promoter/management profiles).
   - It is evidenced in the document's full parsed layout/tables or marked by domain discovery as a standard attribute for that document type (`required: false` by default on `N = 1` industry leaves).
   `DocInduceRunner` excludes generic document titles and filenames from `sampleEntityTokens` unless they match actual entity/identifier patterns, preventing document type names (e.g., `"Credit Rating Report"`, `"Bank Statement"`, `"CIBIL Report"`) from being stripped as company names.

4. **Continuous pack evolution (schema widening).** When new documents of an existing induced pack arrive in later mandates or runs and expose additional schedule tables, fields, or label aliases, `PackInducer` widens the pack to a new version (`v2`, `v3`, ...) that preserves all prior paths while adding the newly discovered optional fields/tables and updating `required` flags from cross-document coverage.
